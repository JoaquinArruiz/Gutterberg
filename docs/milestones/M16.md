# M16 — Print features: cut marks, bleed, duplex

Status: **done** (plan approved by the owner; see "As built" at the end). Everything is in the Print tab and the
sheet engine.
The Source tab and its re-space export do not change.

Three finishing features, all drawn from the same data the preview shows:

```
plan_print (paginate) → OutputSheet[] → finish_sheets (new) → OutputSheet[] → export_sheets
                                          ├─ bleed   (strips drawn under the pieces)
                                          ├─ marks   (lines on top)
                                          └─ duplex  (a back sheet after each front sheet)
```

Geometry lives only in `card-core`, in a new `crates/card-core/src/finish.rs`. The frontend sends
settings and draws what comes back (lines, placements, labels); it computes no positions.
`compute_sheets`, `validate_print` and `export_print` all run `finish_sheets`, so the preview and the
file cannot disagree. With every feature off the result is exactly today's, byte for byte (tested).

## What each feature does

### Cut marks

Lines on the front sheets that show where to cut. Vector strokes in the content stream, never an
image.

- **Style** `off` (default) | `ticks` | `gaps`.
  - `ticks`: short lines **in the margins**, outside the block of pieces. For every distinct left and
    right edge of a piece there is a tick above and below the block; for every top and bottom edge,
    a tick left and right of it. This marks inner cuts too, even when pieces touch (gap 0).
  - `gaps`: a full line down the **middle of each gap** between pieces, across the block, plus a short
    extension into the margins. A gap narrower than the line's width + 0.5 mm gets no line (the line
    would sit on the art); the inspector says so and suggests ticks.
- **Line width** 0.25 mm (range 0.05 to 2). **Colour** `#000000` (DeviceRGB, hex in the file).
  **Tick length** 3 mm (1 to 10). **Distance** 1 mm (0 to 10): a tick starts this far outside the
  piece's edge **plus the bleed**, so it never lands on the bleed.
- Marks are on front sheets only: on a back sheet they would show through, and the fronts already
  say where to cut.

### Bleed

Art that extends past each piece's edge so a slightly off cut shows no white.

- **Bleed** 0 mm (default, off) to 5 mm. **Source** `mirror` (default) | `source`.
- Pieces keep their size. The bleed is **extra** painted outside the piece's rectangle; it is not
  part of the piece and nothing is scaled to make room.
- **`mirror`** (vector-safe, default): the piece's page form is drawn again around the piece, each
  time clipped to a thin strip and reflected over the edge: 4 edge strips and 4 corner squares, 8 extra
  `Do` per piece, all using the one form already in the file. A pixel `d` mm inside the edge ends up
  `d` mm outside it. Reflection is done in the piece's own (oriented) frame, so rotated sources and
  turned pieces work the same.
- **`source`**: the same form clipped to the piece's rectangle grown by the bleed, so the art that
  is already in the gap of the source PDF is used. It only makes sense when the source gap is at
  least the bleed. The engine measures, per piece, the distance to the nearest other piece on the same
  source page; if it is smaller than the bleed that is a **pre-flight error that blocks export**
  (reported on the page, never fixed by mirroring or shrinking silently). Page edges are not a limit.
- **Order of painting on a sheet**: all bleed strips first, then all pieces, then marks. So if the
  gap is smaller than 2 × the bleed, a neighbour's bleed is covered by the piece and never paints over
  it. That case, and a bleed that crosses the page edge, are **warnings**, not errors (see Warnings).
- The sheet's page size, margins and gaps are untouched. Bleed asks the user to leave room; it never
  moves anything.

### Duplex (backs)

A back sheet after every front sheet (`F1, B1, F2, B2, ...`, ready for a duplex printer).

- **Where backs come from** (no automatic pairing in this milestone):
  1. **Common back**: one piece, chosen in the library with "Use as common back" (a button when
     exactly one piece is selected); it goes behind every front.
  2. **Back per piece**: the Selected pieces section gets a "Back" row with "Pick back…", then a click
     on a piece in the library sets it as the back of the selected pieces (Esc cancels); "No back" /
     "Common back" clear it. Stored in `edits.backs` (piece key → piece key), undoable like turns, and
     renumbered with a deleted freeform piece.
  A piece with neither prints a blank slot on the back sheet. If **no** piece on a sheet has a back,
  no back sheet is made for it.
- **Switch** `duplex.on` false (default). **Flip** `long` (default) | `short`: the paper edge the sheet
  is turned over. The engine turns that into an axis from the sheet's shape: portrait + long edge, or
  landscape + short edge, mirror **left-right**; the other two mirror **top-bottom**.
- **Mirroring**: the back of the piece in slot `(x, y, w, h)` of a `W × H` sheet goes to
  `x' = W - x - w` (left-right) or `y' = H - y - h` (top-bottom), centred on the mirrored slot if its
  size differs. Its turn mirrors with it: left-right gives `-turn`; top-bottom gives `180 - turn` (the
  card is tumbled, so its back is head to foot), so a piece turned 90 on the front still has its back
  right way up when the paper is flipped. The back keeps the front's scale factor.
- **Offset**: `offset_x_mm`, `offset_y_mm` (both 0 by default, -10 to 10, in the **printed** sheet,
  positive = right / down) move the whole content of every back sheet, to correct the printer's drift
  between sides. Any bleed on the backs moves with it.
- **Bleed on backs** follows the bleed setting. **Marks on backs**: none (above).
- A back whose final size differs from its front by more than 0.5 mm is a warning (the cuts won't
  line up).

### Warnings

`OutputSheet.warnings` carries codes the UI translates; none block export except where stated above:
`marks_off_page`, `gap_too_narrow_for_line`, `bleed_overlaps_neighbour`, `bleed_off_page`,
`back_size_differs`. They show as a short amber list under the sheet summary and next to the field
that caused them.

## Settings and defaults

All in the print plan, so they are saved in the project and undo does not touch them (as the rest of
the plan).

| Setting | Default | Range / values |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `marks.style` | `off` | `off`, `ticks`, `gaps` |
| `marks.width_mm` | 0.25 | 0.05 to 2 |
| `marks.color` | `#000000` | `#rrggbb` |
| `marks.length_mm` | 3 | 1 to 10 |
| `marks.offset_mm` | 1 | 0 to 10 |
| `bleed.mm` | 0 | 0 to 5 |
| `bleed.source` | `mirror` | `mirror`, `source` |
| `duplex.on` | false | |
| `duplex.flip` | `long` | `long`, `short` |
| `duplex.offset_x_mm` / `offset_y_mm` | 0 / 0 | -10 to 10 |
| `duplex.common_back` | none | a piece key |
| `edits.backs` | `{}` | piece key to piece key |

Values outside a range are an error in Rust (the same kind as a bad margin) and are clamped by the
fields in the UI, so the engine never sees them from the app.

## Engine (Rust)

- `finish.rs`: `FinishOptions { marks, bleed, duplex }` (serde, all `#[serde(default)]`, defaults
  above), `finish_sheets(sheets, cards, backs, options) -> Result<Vec<OutputSheet>>`.
- `OutputSheet` gains (all `#[serde(default)]`, so old JSON and fixtures still load):
  `side: Front | Back`, `bleed: { mm, source }`, `marks: { width_pt, color, lines: [[x1,y1,x2,y2]] }`
  (points, top-left origin like the rest of `layout`/`sheet`), `warnings`.
- `export.rs`: `export_sheets_into` paints per sheet: bleed (8 clipped, reflected draws per piece, or one
  enlarged clip for `source`), pieces as now, then the marks with `RG` / `w` / `m l S`. Reflection is an
  `Affine` built from the oriented rectangle's edge, composed with `card_transform`; corner squares use two
  reflections. No new XObjects and no images.
- `plan_print_in` / `plan_print_files` take `FinishOptions` and a `backs` map and call `finish_sheets`.
  `validate_sheets_in` adds the `source` bleed check (a new `PageIssue` kind with the page).
- Commands: `compute_sheets`, `validate_print`, `export_print` take `finish` and `backs`; the shared
  JSON fixtures (`print_request*.json`) gain them.

  without `backs`) and `edits.backs`.

## UI

- Three new `CollapsibleSection`s in `PrintInspector`, after Sheet, closed by default and remembered
  through `prefs.inspector.sections` (`print.marks`, `print.bleed`, `print.duplex`): **Cut marks** (style,
  width, colour, length, distance), **Bleed** (mm, source), **Duplex** (switch, flip, X and Y offset, the
  common back with its thumbnail and "Remove", and "Use selected piece as common back").
- Selected pieces section: the "Back" row described above. Library cards that have a back show a small
  back badge; the library's pick mode dims what cannot be picked.
- Sheet preview: marks are drawn from `marks.lines`; the bleed is shown as a tinted, dashed frame around each
  piece (the mirrored art is **not** rendered in the preview); back sheets are labelled "Sheet 1 back" and
  mirrored as the engine placed them. The strip and "Sheet i of N" count front and back sheets.
- Words (glossary): add **front / back** to `CLAUDE.md`'s table. Spanish: **frente / dorso** (the owner chose these), plus *cut marks* = **marcas de corte**, *bleed* =
  **sangrado**, *duplex* = **doble cara**. Every string goes to `en.json` and `es.json`; Rust errors and
  warnings reach the UI as codes like the other errors.
- Preferences: nothing new except the three section ids.

## Tests

  wide enough gaps; marks off the page produce `marks_off_page`; distance includes the bleed; none on backs.

  on an axis-aligned piece and on one rotated 7° and turned 90°; 8 extra draws per piece; `source` clip is the

## Out of scope

- CMYK, spot or registration colours, colour bars, slug text, crop marks on back sheets.
- A bleed that is not a reflection of the edge or the source's own gap (no content-aware fill, no round-corner
  handling); rendering the mirrored bleed in the preview (only a frame); per-piece bleed.
- Pairing backs automatically (next page, same order, name match); that belongs with M17's detection.
- Sheet order "all fronts, then all backs"; printer calibration test pages; booklet or fold imposition.
- Round or custom shapes (pieces stay rectangles).

## Decisions (confirmed by the owner)

1. Backs: a common back plus a per-piece back, no automatic pairing.
2. Bleed defaults to mirror; `source` blocks export when the source gap is smaller than the bleed.
3. Marks are black DeviceRGB, fronts only; ticks in the margins are the default style.
4. Spanish: frente / dorso, marcas de corte, sangrado, doble cara.
5. The finishing settings are part of the print plan (saved in the project, not in undo).

## As built

Where the implementation settled on details the plan left open, or went another way:

- **Engine.** `crates/card-core/src/finish.rs`: `Finishing { options, backs }` and
  `finish_sheets(sheets, documents, finishing) -> (sheets, issues)`. `OutputSheet` gained `side`, `bleed`,
  `marks` and `warnings` (all `#[serde(default)]`, `OutputSheet::new` builds a plain front sheet). With everything
  off `finish_sheets` returns its input untouched and the exported content stream is the same as before (a test
  compares them). `Affine` gained `IDENTITY` and `after`.
- **Marks.** `ticks`: a tick above and below the block at every distinct left and right edge, left and right of it at
  every top and bottom edge, starting `bleed + distance` from the block. `gaps`: one line down the middle of each gap
  between the merged spans of the pieces, from `length` above the block to `length` below it; a gap narrower than
  `width + 0.5 mm`, or pieces that touch, give `gap_too_narrow_for_line` and no line. Marks are `DeviceRGB`
  strokes in the content stream (`RG`, `w`, `m l S`), painted last.
- **Bleed.** `bleed_regions` returns the 8 regions of a mirrored bleed (4 strips, 4 corner squares) or the one grown
  rectangle of `source`, each as a quad in source-page coordinates plus the reflection (built from the piece's own
  oriented edge, so rotated pieces work). The exporter draws, per region, `q <placement cm> <quad W n> <reflection cm>
  /Name Do Q`; all bleed of a sheet first, then the pieces, then the marks. A bleed larger than half of a piece is an
  `invalid_sheet` error (it would read past the opposite edge). `source` measures, per piece, the room to the nearest
  other piece on the same source page (the larger of the horizontal and vertical gaps between their bounding boxes);
  less than the bleed is a `bleed_exceeds_source_gap` page issue that blocks export, and also a warning on the sheet.
  `compute_sheets` (the preview) does not return issues; `validate_print` and `export_print` do.
- **Duplex.** Long edge on a portrait sheet (or short on a landscape one) mirrors left to right, the others top to
  bottom. The back's turn is `-turn` (left-right) or `180 - turn` (top-bottom): backs are read with their top at the
  piece's top edge whichever way the sheet flips, so a front corner and a back corner of the same piece always pair as
  top-left with top-right, which the test checks for all four turns, both flips and both sheet shapes. The back keeps
  the front's scale and is centred on the mirrored slot; the offset is added last. A back sheet gets the bleed
  setting and its own warnings but no marks. A piece with neither its own back nor the common one leaves its slot
  empty, and a sheet where no piece has a back gets no back sheet.
- **Errors.** Values outside their ranges (bleed 0 to 5 mm, mark width 0.05 to 2, length 1 to 10, distance 0 to 10, back
  offsets -10 to 10, colour `#rrggbb`) are `invalid_sheet`; the fields clamp, so the app never sends them. A back
  that names a piece that does not exist is `invalid_sheet` too, but the request builder leaves such backs out first
  (`finishingPayload`), so a stale entry in a project cannot block printing.
- **Commands.** `compute_sheets`, `validate_print` and `export_print` take `finishing` (`plan_print_file(s)` take
  `&Finishing`). The two shared JSON fixtures carry it: `print_request.json` with every feature switched on (the Rust
  test reads it back and finishes the sheets), `print_request_freeform.json` with the defaults.
- **Project file version 2.** `plan.finish` and `edits.backs` (piece key to piece key). `project.rs` has
  `MIGRATIONS = [v1_to_v2]`, which adds both with the defaults to a version 1 file that has a `plan` / `edits` object
  and touches nothing else; saving writes version 2. The Zod schema requires the new keys. `fromProject` clamps the
  ranges and drops backs whose keys are not piece keys, or that point at themselves; whether a piece still exists is
  checked when a request is built. A PDF whose layout is reset on open loses its backs and a common back that
  was one of its pieces.
- **UI.** Three sections after Sheet in the Print inspector (`FinishSections.tsx`: Cut marks, Bleed, Duplex), closed
  by default, remembered like the others (`print.marks`, `print.bleed`, `print.duplex`). Warnings show in the section
  they belong to and, for the sheet on screen, under the sheet bar. The Selected pieces section has the Back row
  ("Pick back…" then a click on a piece in the library, Esc cancels; "Remove back"); library pieces show a "Back"
  badge when they have a back of their own and "Common back" when they are the common one. The pieces' backs live in
  `layout-store.cardEdits.backs`, so Ctrl+Z covers them; they are renumbered when a freeform piece is deleted and
  removed with their PDF. The common back and the other settings are in the print plan (not undoable, as the rest of
  it).
- **Preview.** `SheetOverlay` draws the marks as the engine placed them and a dashed frame around each piece for the
  bleed (the mirrored art is not drawn; it is in the file). Duplex plans say "Sheet 2 of 6 · back" and tag the strip
  thumbnails "2 · back".
- **Words.** `CLAUDE.md` glossary: front / back (frente / dorso), cut marks (marcas de corte), bleed (sangrado);
  duplex is "Doble cara" in Spanish. All strings are in `en.json` and `es.json`, the Spanish ones for the owner to review.
- **Different from the plan.** The library does not dim pieces while a back is being picked (any piece can be picked,
  including one of the selection, which then just clears that piece's back). Backs that point at missing pieces are
  dropped when a request is built, not when the project is opened.
- **Tests.** Rust: `tests/finish.rs` (marks, regions, source gap, warnings, export order and no images, duplex for
  every turn and flip, offsets, missing backs, size warning), `tests/finish_render.rs` (pdfium: mirrored bleed on the
  left, right and corner, white past it, a back in the mirrored slot), `tests/project.rs` (v1 migrates, v2 kept),
  the fixture test in `tests/sheets.rs`. Frontend: `finish.test.ts`, `print-store-finish.test.ts`,
  `components/print/finish.test.tsx`, and additions to `project.test.ts` and the request tests.
- **Not done / limits.** Not driven by hand in the Tauri window while writing this: the colour picker, the real
  look of the frames and marks, and a printed duplex test are covered only by jsdom and pdfium renders. Rendering
  the mirrored bleed in the preview, per-piece bleed, CMYK or registration marks, automatic back pairing and "all
  fronts, then all backs" sheet order are not built (see "Out of scope").
