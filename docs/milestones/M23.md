# M23 — Logo and app icon

**Goal:** the app, the installers and the README use the Gutterberg logo, and it reads well on
light and dark backgrounds and at every icon size. Three variants: the logo as it is (light
backgrounds), a dark variant, and an app icon on a solid plate.

No engine changes. Use pnpm for every package (never npm or npx). Every new string goes into
both `src/locales/en.json` and `es.json`.

## Starting point

- `assets/brand/logo.svg` is the owner's logo, unchanged: two overlapping rounded cards
  (bodies `#1E222A`), one with a 3×3 grid of blue cells (`#4A8FE7`), the other green
  (`#3CC47C`), with a gutter cut between them by a mask. Transparent background.
- The app icons today are placeholders: `src-tauri/icons/32x32.png`, `128x128.png`,
  `icon.png` (listed in `tauri.conf.json` › `bundle.icon`). The About section shows the app
  icon (M22).
- **Problems seen in renders** (checked before this brief was written):
  - The dark card bodies (`#1E222A`) nearly vanish on dark backgrounds: the app's dark theme
    (`#1b1c20`) and GitHub's dark page (`#0d1117`). Only the coloured cells stay visible.
  - At 16–32 px the 3×3 cells blur into a solid block.

## 1. The three variants (`assets/brand/`)

Keep the geometry of `logo.svg` in all of them. Change colours and add a plate, nothing else
unless the owner agrees.

| File | Use | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `logo.svg` | Light backgrounds (README in light mode, light theme) | Nothing: the owner's file |
| `logo-dark.svg` | Dark backgrounds (README in dark mode, dark theme) | Lighter card bodies (start around `#3A4150`) and/or a thin light outline (around `#8A93A3`, 4–6 units at the 512 viewBox); the cells keep their colours. Render both options on `#1b1c20` and `#0d1117` and let the owner pick |
| `app-icon.svg` | The app icon, installers, taskbar, dock | The logo centred on a rounded-square **plate in a bone tone, not pure white**: start with `#F4F1EA` and show `#F2EEE3` next to it. The logo fills about 80% of the plate (macOS-style margin). Square 1024 viewBox |

- If the cells blur at small sizes, add `app-icon-small.svg` for 16 and 32 px: same plate,
  the cards drawn bigger with fewer, larger cells (2×2 or plain coloured faces). Show the
  owner both before adding it.
- **Show the owner a contact sheet first** (each variant at 16, 32, 48, 128, 256 and
  1024 px, on white, `#F4F1EA`, `#1b1c20` and `#0d1117`, plus the macOS dock and Windows
  taskbar sizes) and wait for the pick. Put it in the session's scratchpad, not the repo.

## 2. App icons

- Generate every platform icon from the chosen `app-icon.svg` (or a 1024 px PNG of it) with
  `pnpm tauri icon <source>`. It writes the PNG sizes, `icon.ico` and `icon.icns` into
  `src-tauri/icons/`. Update `bundle.icon` in `tauri.conf.json` to the generated files.
- If there is an `app-icon-small.svg`, render it to 16 and 32 px and replace those sizes
  (the PNGs and the small layers of `icon.ico`). Write the steps in a short script,
  `scripts/make-icons.sh`, so the icons can be made again.
- The window icon on Windows and Linux follows the bundle icons. Check it in `pnpm tauri dev`.

## 3. Inside the app

- **Start screen** (`StartScreen`, M22): the logo above the buttons, `logo.svg` in the light
  theme and `logo-dark.svg` in the dark theme (follow the app's theme setting, not only the
  system's).
- **About** (Preferences): the app icon (with its plate), as now, from the new files.
- `index.html` favicon: the app icon.
- Show the product name from one place, as About already does (Tauri's app name), so the
  rename in M15 changes it everywhere at once. Don't hard-code "Gutterberg" in components.
- Alt text from the language files.

## 4. README

At the top of `README.md`, the logo switching with the reader's theme:

```html
<picture>
  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: dark)" srcset="assets/brand/logo-dark.svg">
  <img src="assets/brand/logo.svg" alt="Gutterberg" width="160">
</picture>
```

M15 rewrites the rest of the README; only the logo goes in here.

## Done when

- The owner has picked the dark variant and the plate tone from rendered images.
- `src-tauri/icons/` holds icons generated from `app-icon.svg`, and `scripts/make-icons.sh`
  makes them again.
- The start screen shows the right logo in both themes; About and the window use the new icon.
- The README shows the light logo on GitHub's light theme and the dark one on the dark theme.
- Every check in `CLAUDE.md` passes.
