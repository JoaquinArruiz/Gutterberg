# 0.14.0 — Editing controls and polish

More control over what is on the page, a few things users asked for, and polish of the help, the updates and the
welcome. Do the parts in this order; each one is a feature branch of its own, merged into `release/0.14.0`.

1. Editing controls on the page
2. Backs from another PDF
3. Gutterfold pairs cut apart
4. Help tips off
5. Updates: the toast, the notes and "Tell me about"
6. The welcome, without black bars
7. The keyboard shortcuts sheet (**last**, so it lists everything above)

## Part 1 — Editing controls on the page

Moving a grid already works: with the Grid region tool (`V`) the region's body is dragged, and Source › Layout has its
X/Y fields with ↑/↓ to nudge. What is missing:

1. **Arrow keys move what is picked.** With a grid region or a freeform piece picked, ←↑→↓ move it by 1 mm (0.05 in
   when the unit is inches), with `Shift` by 10 mm (0.5 in). With nothing picked they change page as today
   (`nextPage` / `previousPage` in `src/lib/shortcuts.ts`). A burst of presses is one undo step. The region and the
   pieces stay inside the page, as when dragging.
2. **Several pieces picked at once.** Today one freeform piece is picked at a time (`selectedCard` in
   `src/stores/editor-store.ts`). Add: `Shift`-click (and `Ctrl/Cmd`-click) adds or removes a piece, `Ctrl/Cmd+A`
   picks every piece of the viewed page, `Esc` picks none. Dragging any of them, the arrow keys and `Delete` act on
   all of them; the Freeform pieces section of the sidebar shows the shared angle and size or "mixed". This matters
   since 0.13.0: turning a 3 × 3 grid into pieces gives nine pieces that are otherwise moved one by one. A box drawn
   around pieces to pick them (rubber band) would clash with drawing a new piece by dragging; leave it out unless it
   only starts with a modifier key.
3. **Duplicate.** `Ctrl/Cmd+D` copies the picked pieces a little down and to the right and picks the copies; `Alt`
   while dragging leaves a copy behind. Copies start with no per-piece edits.
4. **Snapping.** While dragging or resizing a region or a piece, its edges and centre snap to the page's edges and
   centre and to the edges of the other pieces and the region, within a few screen pixels, with a thin guide line
   where it snaps. `Alt` held turns it off for that drag (with step 3, `Alt` at the start of a drag copies and turns
   snapping off; check that this reads well, or use another key for one of them). A switch in the canvas toolbar or
   Preferences turns snapping off for good. Snapping to the edges Detect pieces finds is a later step.
5. **Say how to move things.** A help tip the first time a region is drawn: drag it to move it, the arrow keys to
   nudge. A line in `docs/controls.md` for each control above.
6. **Tests** next to the editor (`card-tool.test.tsx` and a new one for the region): nudging, picking several,
   duplicating, snapping maths (in `src/lib/`, pure), undo.

## Part 2 — Backs from another PDF

Asked for by a user who turns every project into gutterfold with another tool because their printer cannot do
reliable manual duplex. Pairing only looks inside one PDF: `pairPieces` (`src/lib/pairing.ts`) groups the pages by
document and pairs within each. Many games ship the fronts and the backs as two PDFs.

1. A fourth choice in Pair backs automatically…: "Backs in another PDF", with a menu of the project's other PDFs
   (shown only when there are two or more). Page *n* of the fronts pairs with page *n* of the backs, piece by piece,
   with the same mirrored and upside-down switches as the other choices. A backs PDF with one page for all the
   fronts is offered as the common back instead.
2. Pages or pieces that do not match leave those pieces unpaired and say how many, as the other choices do. The
   backs PDF's pieces are not printed as fronts after pairing (their copies go to 0, as with the other choices).
3. No project change: backs are stored by full piece id (`cardIdKey`), which names the document, and the common
   back is a `CardId`. A test saves and reopens a project paired across two PDFs.
4. Tests next to `pairing.ts`; texts in both languages; a line in `docs/controls.md` (Backs).

## Part 3 — Gutterfold pairs cut apart

From the same user's comment: they lay out fronts and backs side by side, "like a gutterfold layout but with no gutter
and no bleed (for rapid builds that aren't double-sided)", and cut them apart. Gutterfold always draws the dashed fold
line and puts cut marks only around the pair (`finish.rs`, the gutterfold step).

1. In Print › Duplex › Backs › Beside the front: "Fold the pairs" (as today) or "Cut them apart". Cut apart draws no
   fold line, puts cut marks between the front and the back too, and the gap at the fold works as a plain gap
   (0 for no gutter). Bleed stays its own setting.
2. A new field in `DuplexOptions`; **project version 6**, with a migration that sets "fold" for older projects.
3. Tests in `crates/card-core/tests/`: no fold line and marks between front and back when cut apart; a version 5
   project opens with "fold".

## Part 4 — Help tips off

Preferences › Help has "Enable all help tips" (it brings back closed tips) but no way to turn them all off.

1. A switch "Show help tips" at the top of the Help tips field, on by default. Off: no help tip and no tour of a
   screen shows (one check in `useHint`, `src/components/ui/HintToast.tsx`); the welcome tour still opens once on the
   first start, and Replay welcome tour still works. Stored as `help.tipsOn` (**preferences version 14**; older files
   take the default).
2. The existing button becomes "Show closed tips again", enabled only when some were closed and the switch is on.
3. A help tip's own close button gets a second, smaller choice: "Don't show tips", which turns the switch off and
   says where to turn it on again.
4. Tests in `help.test.tsx` and next to `hints.ts`.

## Part 5 — Updates: the toast, the notes and "Tell me about"

1. **The toast never installs.** Today its "Update" button installs at once (`installUpdate` from the toast in
   `src/lib/update-actions.ts`) and "What's new" opens Preferences › Updates. Make it one button, "Update…", that
   opens Preferences › Updates (`showUpdates`) with the Install button focused, next to "Skip this version". The
   install happens only from there, where the notes are. Update the tests in `update-actions.test.ts`.
2. **Notes with a maximum height.** `ReleaseNotes` (`src/components/ui/ReleaseNotes.tsx`) scrolls inside a box of
   about 14 lines (for example `max-h-60 overflow-y-auto`), in both places it is used (a newer version, and What's
   new after updating), so a long changelog no longer pushes the buttons out of view.
3. **"Tell me about" as a menu.** The four radio cards become the app's `Select` (`src/components/ui/Select.tsx`)
   with the four choices, and the hint of the chosen one under it, followed by the note that is there today.

## Part 6 — The welcome, without black bars

The bars are in the clips themselves: the masters in `assets/welcome/` are 1920 × 1080, but the picture is the
1440 × 1080 middle (4:3), with black at the sides (`ffmpeg -vf cropdetect` gives `crop=1440:1080:240:0` for all four).

1. **Crop the clips.** In `scripts/encode-welcome.sh`, `FILTER` becomes `crop=1440:1080:240:0,scale=960:720:...`, and
   the script's comments say 960 × 720. Re-run it; the outputs must stay under the script's size budget. (Exporting
   the Figma frames at 4:3 is the cleaner fix for later masters; say so in `assets/welcome/README.md`.)
2. **New layout.** The clip on the left at 4:3, the title, text, step dots and buttons on the right; the dialog wider
   than tall. On a narrow window (below about 720 px) it stacks as today, clip on top. The box size formula in
   `WelcomeDialog.tsx` changes to match; the setup step (language, theme, units) uses the same dialog size, its
   choices on the right side too or across the whole dialog.
3. **The setup step's choices** fit their content instead of stretching across the dialog with the options packed on
   the left (the `Segmented` controls in `WelcomeSetup.tsx`).
4. Update `welcome.test.tsx` where it checks sizes or structure.

## Part 7 — The keyboard shortcuts sheet

`src/lib/shortcuts.ts` is the sheet (`?` and Preferences › Help). After parts 1 to 6:

1. Add: nudge and big nudge (arrows, `Shift`+arrows, "with a region or pieces picked"), pick several
   (`Shift`/`Ctrl`-click), pick all (`Ctrl/Cmd+A`), pick none (`Esc`), duplicate (`Ctrl/Cmd+D`, `Alt`-drag), snapping
   off while dragging.
2. Change: next / previous page with the arrows now says "with nothing picked".
3. Check every entry against the code (each `keydown` handler in `src/`) and against `docs/controls.md`, both ways:
   nothing in the code missing from the sheet, nothing in the sheet the code no longer does.
4. Texts in both languages; `shortcuts.test.tsx` covers the new entries.

## Out of scope

- Rubber-band picking without a modifier key, grouping pieces, aligning or spacing several pieces evenly.
- Snapping to the edges Detect pieces finds.
- Moving pieces on the sheets in the Print tab (the engine places them).

## Done when

A turned-into-pieces grid can be picked whole, nudged and duplicated; a fronts PDF and a backs PDF pair in one step;
gutterfold pairs can be cut apart with no fold line; help tips can be turned off; the update toast only opens
Preferences; the notes scroll; the welcome has no black bars and shows the clip beside the text; the shortcuts sheet
matches the code; every check in `AGENTS.md` passes.
