# M18 — Freeform cards

Note (M21): the UI now says "Source" for the Cards tab and "pieces" for cards, and every text lives in `src/locales/`. This brief is kept as it was written.

**Goal:** a messy scan works. Cards of different sizes, not lined up and scanned at an
angle can each be selected, straightened, turned to face the same way, sorted, sized and
printed.

Do this after M13. The engine is already prepared: `OrientedRect` sources, the `Freeform`
`CardId` variant (M11), and per-card `turn` and `scale` in `paginate` / `export_sheets`
(M12). This milestone is mostly UI.

## Scope

- **Card tool (Cards stage).** A second selection tool next to the grid tool: draw one
  rectangle per card. Each rectangle can be moved, resized and **rotated**: a rotate handle
  above the top edge, plus an angle field in the sidebar (Shift snaps to 15°). The
  magnifier works on its corner handles. Store them in the page's `freeform` group as
  `OrientedRect`s (normalized in the UI, converted only through `coordinates.ts`).
- **Mixing on one page.** A page can have a grid group, a freeform group, or both (for
  example, a 3×3 grid plus one odd-sized card below it).
- **Straightening.** The source angle is undone on export, so a card drawn at 7° comes out
  straight. The preview shows the card straightened.
- **Turn (Print stage).** In the card library, rotate a card by 90° steps (buttons and
  `R` / `Shift+R`). "Make all portrait" and "Make all landscape" act on the selection.
- **Sort.** Drag cards in the card library to reorder them. The plan's order (Grouped /
  Interleaved) follows this order.
- **Set real size.** Per card or per selection: enter the real size in mm (for example
  63 × 88), or a percentage. The app stores it as `scale`. Show the result as
  "63.0 × 88.0 mm (98.4%)" wherever card sizes appear, and mark scaled cards in the library
  with a small badge, so a scale is never hidden.
- Undo/redo (from M11) covers every edit above.

## Out of scope

- Photos taken at an angle (perspective correction). It would need warping the image,
  which means rasterising. The user should scan the cards instead.
- Automatic detection of card outlines (M17).

## Done when

- A test PDF with cards of three sizes, each tilted by a few degrees and one upside down,
  exports with every card straight, facing the same way and at its source size.
- Setting "63 × 88 mm" on a card that measures 64 × 89.4 mm exports it at 63 × 88 mm, and
  the library shows its percentage.
- Dragging cards in the library changes their order on the sheets.
- Grid groups behave exactly as before.

## As built

Where the implementation settled on details the brief left open:

- **Where freeform cards live.** Not as a group kind: `layout-store.freeform` is a map from page
  to its `OrientedRect[]` (normalized), next to `groups`, so a page can have a grid and freeform
  cards at once. The reserved `FreeformGroup` kind is gone from the UI's `PageGroup`. A card's
  identity is its position on the page (`CardId::Freeform.index`), as the engine numbers them.
  Skipped pages keep their cards but they are left out of every plan.
- **Request.** `toRustGroups` sends a page's freeform cards as a one-page `freeform` group right
  after that page's grid cards, splitting the grid group around such pages, so the library is in
  page order. `crates/card-core/tests/data/print_request_freeform.json` is the second shared
  fixture (grid and two tilted freeform cards, one turned, one scaled, reordered); the frontend
  builds it and `sheets.rs` plans from it.
- **Per-card edits** (`lib/card-edits.ts`, `layout-store.cardEdits`): `turns`, `scales` and a
  full `order` of card keys. They sit in the undoable part of the layout store, so Ctrl+Z covers
  turn, size, sort, and drawing or deleting a card, each as one step. Copies stay in the plan
  (print store) and are not undoable, as before.
- **Deleting a freeform card** renumbers the later ones: their turn, scale and place in the order
  move with them in the same undo step, and the print store's copies and library selection follow
  through `onFreeformCardDeleted`. Undoing the delete brings the card back but not its copies.
- **Engine change** (the only Rust change besides tests): `plan_sheets` prints cards in the order of
  the `settings` list; cards without a setting follow, once each, in page order. The frontend lists
  every card in the user's order whenever the plan is custom or any card is turned, scaled or
  moved (`planSettings`); "all cards" with nothing edited still sends no settings.
- **"Same as source" needs the planner's absence**: with freeform cards, or any turned, resized or
  reordered card, the sheet grid falls back to Auto (the inspector says so). Same-as-source would
  drop freeform cards and ignore per-card settings.
- **Card tool.** Tool id `card`, key `C`; the old Select tool is labelled "Grid region" (`V`).
  Draw by dragging on the page (a click or a drag under about 3 mm makes nothing, and a click
  clears the picked card), drag the body to move, drag a handle to resize (measured along the
  card's own edges, so a tilted card resizes as it looks; the opposite edge stays), drag the dot 24
  px above the top edge to rotate (the top edge follows the pointer; Shift snaps to 15°). `Delete`
  or "Delete card" removes the picked card, `Esc` drops the pick. The picked card is session
  state in `editor-store`. A whole drag is one undo step. In the grid tool the cards are shown but
  take no pointer events, and in the card tool the grid region is a backdrop that takes none.
- **Magnifier.** Holding any handle of a card opens the same ×4 loupe and quarter-speed drag; it
  follows the handle's rotated position and outlines the card (`Magnifier`'s `card` prop).
- **Sidebar.** A "Freeform cards" section (Cards stage) shows the page's cards and, for the picked
  one, an Angle field (-180..180, wraps), Width and Height in the user's unit, and Delete. The page
  thumbnails' badge adds the card count ("3×3 + 2", "2 cards").
- **Straightening in previews** is done in CSS (`CardImage`), not by a new renderer: the crop of the
  source's bounding box is rotated back by the source angle, cut to the card and turned, in the
  library and on the sheet preview. The export does the same with PDF matrices (M12) and nothing is
  rasterised there.
- **Turn.** `R` / `Shift+R` (while the Print stage is up and no field has focus) and two buttons turn
  the selection by 90°. "Make all portrait / landscape" turns the selected cards that are the wrong
  way round by a quarter turn; a card already turned a quarter is turned back rather than on, so it
  never ends upside down. An upside-down scan can be drawn the right way up and turned 180°.
- **Sort.** Drag in the library (pointer events, not the browser's drag and drop, which a webview
  may intercept): the selection moves if the pressed card is in it, otherwise just that card, to
  before or after the card under the pointer (left or right half); the list scrolls near its edges.
  With a filter on, the cards move within the full order.
- **Real size.** The "Selected cards" section of the Print inspector has Width and Height in the
  user's unit (for cards with the same source size), a Scale in % (10 to 500) and "Back to the size
  on the page". Width or height sets one uniform scale, so the card keeps its shape. A scale shows
  as "63.0 × 88.0 mm (98.4%)" in that section and in a card's tooltip, and as an amber badge on
  the card in the library.
- **Cards stage export** is unchanged: it re-spaces grids. Freeform cards are printed from the Print
  stage, and a page with only freeform cards is reported before export ("print it from the Print
  stage") instead of being exported empty.
- **Hint.** `freeform-tool` (M20 catalog) shows the first time the card tool is used.
- **Tests.** `freeform.test.ts` and `card-edits.test.ts` (geometry, order, scale, delete renumbering),
  `card-tool.test.tsx` (draw, move, resize, rotate, snap, magnifier, sidebar), `card-edits.test.tsx`
  (turn, size, sort, undo), `layout-store-freeform.test.ts`, request tests for groups, order and the
  fixture; in Rust, order, grid plus freeform on one page, three tilted sizes straight and facing
  one way, 64 × 89.4 → 63 × 88 mm, and a pdfium render of a tilted card right way up and turned 180°.
- **Not done / limits.** Not driven by hand in the Tauri window while writing this: the pointer
  feel (hit sizes, the loupe on a tilted card, drag-to-sort scrolling) is only covered by jsdom
  tests. Straightened previews are as sharp as the crop of the bounding box. Perspective
  correction and automatic detection stay out of scope.
