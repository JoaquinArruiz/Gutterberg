### Added

- CMYK export for print shops, and trim and bleed boxes on sheets of one piece.
- Crop for sleeves: cut the same amount off every piece of a grid.
- Zoom and move round the sheet on the Print tab.
- Remove or skip several pages at once, and copies from a pasted list.
- A 50 mm scale check on each sheet, and a FAQ for common questions.

### Changed

- Less used Print settings fold under More options.
- Faster with big decks. Projects are saved as version 9.
